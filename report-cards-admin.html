Report Cards

BEFORE YOU START

Report cards come from the MBA Report Creator

MBA Plugins → MBA Report Creator → Run Reports. Running and printing them never changes a grade.

WHERE

The left menu → MBA Plugins → MBA Report Creator → Run Reports.

WHAT YOU NEED

Which quarter, and which students — one student, a homeroom, your caseload, or all of Summit.

HOW LONG

A minute to set up. A big group takes a little while to load.

GOOD TO KNOW
🔒Generate and Print never change anything. Run them as often as you like.
📅One report card per quarter. Pick the one that matches the quarter you're printing. New quarters show up in the Report list when they're ready.
STEP 1 OF 5 · OPTIONAL

Pick your students first (or skip it)

  1. 1
    On the Start Page, pick the students you want cards for — one student, a homeroom, or your caseload under Stored Searches.
  2. 2
    They show up under Current Student Selection.
  3. 3
    Printing for all of Summit? Skip this step.
Not sure how? Search the student by last name on the Start Page, or open Stored Searches for a saved group.
STEP 2 OF 5

MBA Plugins → MBA Report Creator → Run Reports

  1. 1
    In the left menu, click MBA Plugins, then MBA Report Creator.
  2. 2
    Under Functions, click Run Reports.
  3. 3
    The page is called Report Creator Reports.
STEP 3 OF 5

Fill in the one row

  1. 1
    Report: the report card for the quarter — for example SSNYK Report Card - Q1 2026-27.
  2. 2
    Student Selection: Current Selection = the students from step 1 (the number shows how many). All Students in Summit School at Nyack = everyone.
  3. 3
    Grade Level: All Grades. Sort Order: Student Name, or Home Room to print by homeroom.
  4. 4
    Leave Include Dropped Courses and Include Transferred Out Students unchecked.
STEP 4 OF 5

Click Generate Reports

  1. 1
    The blue Generate Reports button is at the end of the row.
  2. 2
    The cards open in a new tab.
  3. 3
    A big group takes a little while to load — wait for the buttons to show up in the box at the top left.
STEP 5 OF 5

Click Print, then pick a printer or Save as PDF

  1. 1
    In the box at the top left, click Print.
  2. 2
    In the print window, Layout should already say Landscape and Margins Default.
  3. 3
    Pick your printer — or change Destination to Save as PDF.
GOOD TO KNOW
✋Skip Email and Archive Reports. Nicole saves each quarter's cards once — Email isn't used at Summit.
👆Double-click a card to jump to that student's pages in PowerSchool.
✎️Wrong grade or comment on a card? Ask the teacher to fix it in PowerTeacher Pro, then generate the card again. Still wrong? Reach out.
