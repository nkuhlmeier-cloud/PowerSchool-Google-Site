THE ICONS  —  TAP ONE TO SEE WHAT IT MEANS
The little icons by a student's name are alerts — each one is a heads-up about that student. In PowerSchool, tap the icon and a pop-up tells you more. Summit uses seven: (plus PowerSchool's own ).

School vs. Home: Name & Pronouns

▾
On every student. Tap it before anything goes home — a progress report, a report card, a conference, a phone call. The pop-up has two rows: the name and pronouns the student uses at school, and the name and pronouns to use with family. “Same as at school” means there is no difference.
For everyday class use you do not need the alert at all — the header already shows GOES BY and PRONOUNS.
Who enters it: the social workers.
Where: student → More → Student Custom Screens → Name & Pronouns (School vs. Home). Preferred First Name, Preferred Last Name and Pronouns feed the header (GOES BY, GOES BY (LAST), PRONOUNS); the two With Family fields feed the pop-up — fill both whenever anything family-facing differs, then Submit. Subs never see this one.

IEP Accommodations & Modifications

▾
Shows once the student's IEP has been entered. Tap it for the testing accommodations — one per row with the detail that matters (double time, breaks, tests read, calculator…) — and the program modifications & accommodations, plus the date of the IEP they come from.
Where it lives: student → More → Student Custom Screens → IEP Accommodations & Modifications (that screen is exactly what the pop-up shows). The full IEP file is under Student Profile → Attachments. Out of date after a new IEP? Email Nicole.

C&C — Close & Continuous supervision

▾
A staff member stays with this student at all times. The student also carries the red card. If you have this student and nobody is with them, say something right away. Tap the icon for the note and dates.
Who sets it: the campus coordinators, when administration or the clinical team decide a student needs it.
Where: student → Student Profile → MBA Alert Creator → New Alert → pick the type, type the note staff should read, add an Expires date if it is temporary (blank = never) → Save. To end one early, set its Expires date on the same page (steps below).

ESC — Escort

▾
Staff walk the student between places in the building — the student does not travel alone. The student carries the blue card. Tap the icon for the note and dates.
Who sets it: the campus coordinators, when administration or the clinical team decide a student needs it.
Where: student → Student Profile → MBA Alert Creator → New Alert → pick the type, type the note staff should read, add an Expires date if it is temporary (blank = never) → Save. To end one early, set its Expires date on the same page (steps below).

AS — Administrative Support

▾
A longer-term coverage arranged by administration (no card for this one). Tap the icon to read what it means for this student; check with the student's social worker if you are not sure what it means for your class.
Who sets it: the campus coordinators, when administration decides a student needs it.
Where: student → Student Profile → MBA Alert Creator → New Alert → pick the type, type the note staff should read, add an Expires date if it is temporary (blank = never) → Save. To end one early, set its Expires date on the same page (steps below).

Absences + Cuts (does not incl excused)

▾
Appears once a student has 5 or more unexcused absences and cuts (codes A, CC, CG) in one class this year. Tap it to see which class, the teacher and the count. Summit's rule is per class: at 5, 10 and 15 the TA emails the student's team (homeroom teacher, guidance counselor, social worker); 15 = no credit for that course.
Comes from: attendance, automatically — nothing to enter. It only goes away if the attendance is corrected (an excused day is EA, which does not count).

WBL — Work Schedule

▾
This student has work-based learning on their schedule — a job site or Animal Care during one of the WB blocks. Tap the icon to see where they go, which day and periods, and the staff member who runs it — for example Walgreens — Tue, periods 2-4 | Shaina Rochester. During that block the student is at work, not in class.
Comes from: the schedule, automatically — nothing to enter. It appears when a WB-block section is on the student's schedule and goes away when that section is dropped. Wrong site or day? That is a schedule fix — Shaina Rochester (WBL staff) or email Nicole.

Counselors (the three people)

▾
Not an alert — it is PowerSchool's own icon, up by the student's name on every student. Tap it for the student's counselor. The pin next to it (Locate) shows where the student is right now according to their schedule.

ADD OR END A COVERAGE ALERT  —  CAMPUS COORDINATORS
C&C, Escort and Admin Support are manual alerts — someone puts the alert on the student when the coverage starts and ends it when the coverage stops. Nothing fills them in by itself.
1

Open the student's MBA Alert Creator page

▾
Search the student, then Student Profile → MBA Alert Creator. The top box, MBA Manual Alerts, lists the coverages the student has right now (none = empty list).
📷 picture 4 below
2

Add one — New Alert, fill the row, Save

▾
Click New Alert. In the new row: Alert Type → AS, C&C or ESC. Alert → one line staff should read when they tap the icon (why, and anything they must do). Activation → leave blank to start today. Expires → the last day of the coverage, or leave blank if there is no end date yet. Click Save. Teachers and subs see the icon on the student right away.
📷 picture 5 below
3

End one — give it an Expires date

▾
Same page: click the pencil on the alert's row, put today's date in Expires and click Save. The icon disappears and the alert moves under Show Expired Alerts, so there is still a record of it. Delete removes it with no record — only for one added by mistake. Remember the Daily Notes list is separate — update that too.

1

NAME · GOES BY · GOES BY (LAST) · PRONOUNS

▾
NAME is the student's name in PowerSchool (Last, First). GOES BY is the first name the student uses at school — use this one; GOES BY (LAST) is the last name they use. PRONOUNS are the student's pronouns at school. (What to use with the family is in the pink alert.) A blank field is simply not shown, so some students have fewer labels.
2

YEAR LEVEL · STUDENT # · SCHOOL

▾
Admin side only. YEAR LEVEL is PowerSchool's grade-level slot — at Summit it counts the student's year at Summit (1 = first year), not a school grade. STUDENT # is the student's number — the same as the state number now. SCHOOL is SSNYK for every active student.
3

DOB · AGE

▾
DOB is the date of birth; AGE is worked out from it (years and months).
4

HR · COUNSELOR · SW · SPEECH · PSYCHIATRIST

▾
The people on the student's team: HR = homeroom teacher, COUNSELOR = guidance counselor, SW = social worker, SPEECH = speech therapist, PSYCHIATRIST. These are who you loop in when something comes up with the student. SW, SPEECH and PSYCHIATRIST are set on the student's Related Service Providers custom screen; the counselor is PowerSchool's own counselor assignment.
5

PLACEMENT · COTTAGE · DISTRICT

▾
PLACEMENT = Day or Resident. COTTAGE = the cottage a residential student lives in (blank for day students). DISTRICT = the student's home school district — the one that placed them at Summit. Placement and cottage are set on the Placement Status custom screen.
GOOD TO KNOW
👁️Same icons on both sides. Teachers see them in PowerTeacher, the admin side sees them on the student pages — one source, no separate lists.
🤝Subs see the header (GOES BY, PRONOUNS) and the IEP and coverage icons, but not the pink Name & Pronouns alert.
📋Today's C&C / Escort / Admin Support lists are also in the Daily Notes — the icon is the per-student version.
