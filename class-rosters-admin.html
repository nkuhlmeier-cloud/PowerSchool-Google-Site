BEFORE YOU START

Print a class list for any teacher

Class Rosters (PDF) makes a clean, printable list of the students in a teacher's classes. Our saved roster, Summit Teacher Roster, sets up the look for you — you just pick the teacher.

WHERE

Data and Reporting → Reports → System Reports → Class Rosters (PDF).

WHAT IT PRINTS

For each class: the teacher, class, period and number of students, then the student names.

HOW LONG

About a minute. The PDF is usually ready right away.

GOOD TO KNOW
👁️Look & print only. Running a roster doesn't change anything in PowerSchool.
✉️No System Reports in your menu? Reach out — contact info is at the bottom of this page.
STEP 1 OF 8

Open System Reports

  1. 1
    Sign in with the ADMIN PORTAL SIGN-IN button on the Admin Portal page of this site.
  2. 2
    In the left menu, click Data and Reporting — a menu pops out.
  3. 3
    Click Reports, then System Reports.
STEP 2 OF 8

Click Class Rosters (PDF)

  1. 1
    Scroll down to the Student/Staff Listings section.
  2. 2
    Click Class Rosters (PDF) — “Class information and roll sheets.”
STEP 3 OF 8

Load the Summit Teacher Roster

  1. 1
    At the top right, open the Load Roster list and pick Summit Teacher Roster.
  2. 2
    That fills in the heading, the Student Name column, the fonts and the page setup. Leave those as they are.
GOOD TO KNOW
💾The roster saves the look only — not the teacher or the classes. You pick those each time (next two steps).
STEP 4 OF 8

Pick the teacher

  1. 1
    In Print rosters for, click the teacher's name. Names are last name first.
  2. 2
    More than one teacher? Hold ⌘ Command (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows) and click each name.
STEP 5 OF 8

All of the teacher's classes, or just one?

  1. 1
    All classes: leave every Meeting(s) box unchecked.
  2. 2
    Just one class: check the boxes in that class's period row — like the LR1 row in the picture.
  3. 3
    Leave Include students who on are currently enrolled in class.
GOOD TO KNOW
☑️Boxes already checked when you open it? PowerSchool keeps the Meeting(s) boxes from the last run. Uncheck them to print every class.
STEP 6 OF 8

Scroll down and click Submit

  1. 1
    Leave everything else alone. When to print stays on ASAP.
  2. 2
    Click Submit at the bottom right.
  3. 3
    Don't use Save/Edit Roster Set. It's for changing or deleting the saved Summit Teacher Roster — for everyone.
STEP 7 OF 8

Open the PDF from the Report Queue

  1. 1
    After Submit, PowerSchool takes you to the Report Queue. When the Status says Completed, click View.
  2. 2
    Not done yet? Wait a few seconds and click Refresh.
GOOD TO KNOW
🕐Finished jobs clear out of the Report Queue on their own after a while. Gone? Just run it again.
STEP 8 OF 8

Print it

  1. 1
    The roster opens as a PDF: a heading for each class (teacher, class, period, number of students), then the student names.
  2. 2
    Click the printer icon at the top right, or press ⌘ Command + P (Mac) / Ctrl + P (Windows).
GOOD TO KNOW
📑Picked several teachers or classes? They all come in the same PDF, each class with its own heading.
