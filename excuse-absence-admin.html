ABOUT 1 MINUTESOCIAL WORKERS · COUNSELORSCODE: EA
HOW TO DO IT  —  TAP A STEP
1

Sign in to the Admin Portal

▾
Use the ADMIN PORTAL SIGN-IN button on the Admin Portal page of this site. You land on the Start Page.
2

Find the student

▾
On the Start Page, type the student's last name in the search bar. Click the student's name to open their student profile.
📷 picture 1 below
3

Click Attendance, then pick the week

▾
  • In the student's left-hand menu click Attendance — in the menu that pops out, click Attendance Overview; the whole term with one column per week.

  • This week: click Modify Attendance in the menu that pops out next to Attendance.

  • An earlier week: click the blue dates of that week at the top of the grid (for example 9/14-9/18).

    • 📷 pictures 2 and 3 below
4

Pick the code: EA

▾
Under Current attendance code choose EA (Excused Absent). Want to add the reason? Click the little note button in the class box and type a Comment.
📷 picture 4 below
5

Click Set All under the day, then Submit

▾
Under the date you are excusing, click Set All — every period of that day turns EA. Excusing only part of the day? Click the single periods instead. Then scroll to the bottom and click Submit. It stays gray until you have changed something.
📷 pictures 4 and 5 below
GOOD TO KNOW
🔒EA is an admin-side code. Teachers cannot enter it — when they see EA on their sheet, they know a social worker excused the student.
⛔Do not use “Change Meeting Attendance.” It throws a security error for our accounts. Always go through Modify Attendance.
📅Daily view is turned off at Summit — attendance is by period (Meeting). Set All is how you cover a whole day.
