BEFORE YOU START

Every period, every class, under a minute

Attendance is taken by period in PowerSchool, from the teacher Start Page. Four clicks: open the class, pick a code for anyone who isn't there, Submit.

WHERE

The Start Page you land on after signing in — the chair icon next to each class.

WHEN

AM homeroom, periods 1–4, then 6–7. No attendance at lunch. PM homeroom is a headcount.

WHAT YOU NEED

Where every rostered student actually is. The code card is in step 5 — and on the back of the card from conference day.

GOOD TO KNOW
🚨Know where every rostered student is — if someone is unaccounted for, communicate immediately.
🔒EA, BD, ISR and OSS are entered for you by the campus coordinator and social workers, and locked for staff — if one is there, leave it.
STEP 1 OF 4

Sign in to the teacher portal

  1. 1
    Use the TEACHER PORTAL SIGN-IN button on the Teacher Portal page of this site — never a Googled login link.
  2. 2
    You land on the Start Page: one row per class.
  3. 3
    Password not working? Reach out to Jon or Nicole to reset it.
STEP 2 OF 4

Click the chair icon next to the class meeting now

The chair is the Take Attendance button — one per class. It grays out on days a class doesn't meet.

  1. 1
    Find the class that is meeting right now in the list.
  2. 2
    Click the chair in the Take Attendance column for that row.
  3. 3
    A green check on the chair = today's attendance is already in. A dotted circle = not yet.
STEP 3 OF 4

Pick a code for anyone who isn't in class

Everyone starts as Present. Next to anyone who isn't in the room, pick the code for where they actually are.

  1. 1
    Click the code box next to the student's name and pick the code: A L AP WORK CG CC WS IH and so on.
  2. 2
    Not sure which one? The whole card is in step 5 · The codes, including the What do I put? column.
  3. 3
    If EA, BD, ISR or OSS is already showing, it was entered for you — leave it.
GOOD TO KNOW
🧭Not in class? Did an adult take them or OK it? Yes = WS (or IH in homeroom). No = it's a cut: CG, or CC in the cottage.
📋WBL blocks start blank on purpose. Confirm the student is at the worksite before coding WORK.
STEP 4 OF 4

Submit — even if everyone is present

Nothing is recorded until you click Submit. Do it every period, even when nobody is out.

  1. 1
    Click Submit.
  2. 2
    Back on the Start Page the chair turns green — that period is done.
  3. 3
    Made a mistake? Click the chair again, fix the code, Submit again. Same-day only — an older date needs a fix from the office, so reach out.
GOOD TO KNOW
📅Attendance edits are same-day only. Need an earlier date fixed? Reach out — contact info is at the bottom of this page.

COMMON QUESTIONS · TAP ONE

If something doesn't look right

1The chair is gray and I can't click it.
That class doesn't meet today (or not at this time). Attendance is only open for a class on the days it meets.
2A student shows EA / BD / ISR / OSS and I can't change it.
Those codes are entered by the campus coordinator and social workers and are locked for staff. Leave them — it means the office already knows.
3A student is in my room but should be at work (WBL).
Mark what you see: Present. Shaina Rochester (WBL staff) handles the work side.
4I submitted the wrong code yesterday.
Edits are same-day only. Reach out with the student, the date, the period and the right code, and it gets fixed for you.
5Nobody is out — do I still Submit?
Yes. Submit is what records the period. Until you click it, the chair stays open.
6Where's PM homeroom?
PM homeroom is a headcount, not a PowerSchool attendance period. Summit's day in PowerSchool is AM homeroom, periods 1–4, then 6–7.
