WHAT'S DUE
THE BIG PICTURE

One ICSP per student, for their whole time at Summit

Every student has one ICSP in PowerSchool. You never make a second one — you update the same one, and PowerSchool keeps the old versions for you.

THE PLAN

A folder with a sticky note.

  • The blue card on the Student Support tab.
  • The sticky note = Start Date and Expires.
  • Expires is just the date the next review is due. Nothing happens when it passes.
THE ICSP FORM

The paper inside the folder.

  • The link on the card: Individual Crisis Support Plan (ICSP).
  • Fill it in once, then update the same form at each review.
  • Everyone who opens it sees the newest version.
RESPONSE HISTORY

The old versions, kept for you.

  • Every time you click Submit, PowerSchool saves that version.
  • Admission → first Tx conference → every 6 months: all there.
  • Open them from the gray bar at the top of the form.
AT ADMISSION
Create the plan + write the ICSP (steps 1–4).
~30 DAYS LATER
First Tx conference → update it (steps 5–8).
EVERY 6 MONTHS
Review → update it again (steps 5–8).
CREATE · STEP 1 OF 4

Open the student's Student Support tab

  1. 1
    Find the student from the Start Page search bar and open their profile.
  2. 2
    In the left menu click Student Profile → Forms → Student Forms.
  3. 3
    Click the Student Support tab.
  4. 4
    Blank page? Reload it — this tab sometimes needs a second try.
CREATE · STEP 2 OF 4

Click New and fill in the Add a New Plan box

  1. 1
    Click the green New button.
  2. 2
    Type: Individual Crisis Support Plan (ICSP).
  3. 3
    Start Date: today.
  4. 4
    Expiration Date: the date of the first Tx conference — about 30 days out.
  5. 5
    Click Save changes. A blue plan card appears.
CREATE · STEP 3 OF 4

Open the form, fill it in, click Submit

  1. 1
    On the right side of the card, click Individual Crisis Support Plan (ICSP).
  2. 2
    Fill in the ICSP.
  3. 3
    Scroll to the bottom and click the blue Submit.
  4. 4
    Save for Later is a draft only you can see. Nobody else sees the ICSP until you Submit.
CREATE · STEP 4 OF 4

Check the card says Active

  1. 1
    Back on the Student Support tab, the card should show a green Active badge.
  2. 2
    If it says Inactive, click Activate this Plan on the card.
  3. 3
    Done. The student now has their one ICSP.
UPDATE · STEP 1 OF 4

Open the same ICSP (not New)

  1. 1
    Student Support tab → click Individual Crisis Support Plan (ICSP) on the card.
  2. 2
    Don't click New — that would make a second plan.
UPDATE · STEP 2 OF 4

Change what changed, then Submit

  1. 1
    The form opens with everything you wrote last time.
  2. 2
    Change only what's different now.
  3. 3
    Click Submit. The old version is kept automatically — no copies, no renaming.
UPDATE · STEP 3 OF 4

Move the two dates: Edit → Save changes

  1. 1
    Back on the card, click the blue Edit button. The Edit Plan box opens.
  2. 2
    Start Date: the date of this review (today).
  3. 3
    Expiration Date: the next review — 6 months from today.
  4. 4
    Click Save changes.
UPDATE · STEP 4 OF 4

Leave it Active

  1. 1
    The plan stays Active the whole time the student is at Summit.
  2. 2
    Never click Deactivate this Plan — that is the only thing that turns a plan off. The date never does.
RESPONSE HISTORY · STEP 1 OF 2

Open the ICSP and click the gray bar at the top

  1. 1
    The bar says “There are 2 previous responses to this form.” (the number = how many older versions there are).
  2. 2
    Clicking it opens the Form Options box.
RESPONSE HISTORY · STEP 2 OF 2

Pick a date under Response History, then Load Response

  1. 1
    The Response History list shows one line per Submit, newest first: the 6-month update, the Tx-conference update, the admission ICSP.
  2. 2
    Pick the one you want and click Load Response to read it.
  3. 3
    Just look. Don't Submit while an old version is loaded — click Cancel or reload the page to get back to the current one.
WHAT'S DUE · THE RENEWAL DASHBOARD

Open the ICSP Renewal Dashboard and pick your name

  1. 1
    Open summitnyack.powerschool.com/admin/summit_icsp_alerts.html (bookmark it) while signed in to the Admin Portal — it is also under Summit Tools in the left menu.
  2. 2
    Under Social worker, pick your name.
  3. 3
    Overdue = the review date has passed. Due in 14 days = coming up. Update those ICSPs, then move the dates (steps 5–8).
  4. 4
    No ICSP on file = a student who still needs their first one.
GOOD TO KNOW
📅“Expires” only means “review due.” When the date passes, the plan does not turn off, does not disappear, and teachers can still see it. The student just shows up on the dashboard.
⛔Only Deactivate this Plan turns a plan off. Don't click it. If a plan ever shows Inactive, click Activate this Plan.
📄One ICSP per student, always the same one. No copies, no new plans, no renaming. Submit keeps every version for you.
👤Teachers read the PowerSchool copy. View Student ICSP on their Start Page opens the newest version — so an update you don't Submit here is an update nobody sees.
🔄Student Support tab came up blank? Reload the page. It is a PowerSchool glitch, not you.
✉️Something looks wrong on a plan? Reach out — contact info is at the bottom of this page.
