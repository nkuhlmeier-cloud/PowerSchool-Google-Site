Excuse an Absence (EA)

SOCIAL WORKERS · COUNSELORSCODE: EAABOUT 1 MINUTEOPEN FULL SCREEN ↗
BEFORE YOU START

EA is the admin-side code for an excused absence

Teachers can't enter it — when they see EA on their sheet, they know a social worker excused the student. You set it on the student's attendance, one day (or one period) at a time.

WHERE

The student's page → Attendance → Modify Attendance (this week) or the week's blue dates (an earlier week).

WHAT YOU NEED

The student's name, the date(s), and whether it is the whole day or just some periods.

HOW LONG

About a minute per day excused.

GOOD TO KNOW
⛔Do not use “Change Meeting Attendance.” It throws a security error for our accounts. Always go through Modify Attendance.
📅Daily view is turned off at Summit — attendance is by period (Meeting). Set All is how you cover a whole day.
STEP 1 OF 5

Sign in to the Admin Portal

  1. 1
    Use the ADMIN PORTAL SIGN-IN button on the Admin Portal page of this site.
  2. 2
    You land on the Start Page.
STEP 2 OF 5

Find the student

  1. 1
    On the Start Page, type the student's last name in the search bar and press Enter.
  2. 2
    Click the student's name to open their student profile.
STEP 3 OF 5

Click Attendance, then pick the week

  1. 1
    In the student's left-hand menu click Attendance — a menu pops out next to it.
  2. 2
    This week: click Modify Attendance in that pop-out menu.
  3. 3
    An earlier week: click Attendance Overview instead — the whole term, one column per week — then click the blue dates of the week you need at the top of the grid (for example 9/14-9/18).
STEP 4 OF 5

Pick the code: EA

  1. 1
    Under Current attendance code choose EA (Excused Absent).
  2. 2
    Want to add the reason? Click the little note button in the class box and type a Comment.
STEP 5 OF 5

Click Set All under the day, then Submit

  1. 1
    Under the date you are excusing, click Set All — every period of that day turns EA.
  2. 2
    Excusing only part of the day? Click the single periods instead.
  3. 3
    Scroll to the bottom and click Submit. It stays gray until you have changed something.
GOOD TO KNOW
✉️Wrong day or can't find the student? Reach out — contact info is at the bottom of this page.
