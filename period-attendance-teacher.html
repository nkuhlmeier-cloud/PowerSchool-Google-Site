WHAT IT IS
Period Attendance shows one student's attendance for every school day and every period — AM homeroom, 1st–4th, 6th and 7th — and counts up the cuts for you. Same codes as Meeting Attendance, just easier to read. It's look-only: nothing on this page changes attendance.
HOW TO OPEN IT  —  TAP A STEP
1

Start Page → Student Information

▾
On your Start Page, find the class and click the little ID-card icon in the Student Information column. The students in that class come up.
2

Click the student's name

▾
Their student page opens — the name, the header and the alert icons across the top.
3

Select Screens → Period Attendance

▾
At the top of the student's page, open the Select Screens menu and pick Period Attendance. It sits right under Meeting Attendance.

WHAT YOU'LL SEE  —  TAP A PART
1

The dates — From, To, All dates, Print

▾
It opens on the whole term so far. Change From and To to look at just a stretch — since the last meeting, say. All dates puts it back. Print prints just the report, not the PowerSchool menus.
2

The Summary and two charts

▾
A few short lines that say it for you: days in school, which days were absent (excused or not), how many class periods were cut and when — before lunch, after lunch or all over — whether the cuts were CC (stayed in the cottage) or CG (on campus, not in class), AM homeroom cuts and lates. Under it: Class Periods Cut by Week and Cuts by Period — is it getting better or worse, and is it always the same period?
Sample: the test student, ZZTest, Student. Every student's page looks the same.
3

Codes by Day — every day, every period

▾
One row per school day, grouped by week. One column per period: AM HR, 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 6th, 7th (no 5th — that's lunch).
Cut — CC, CGAbsent — A, OSSExcused — EA, AP, OC, TR, WS, IHLate — L
A blank box = no code entered, which counts as present. A dash = not in that class yet.
Sample: the test student, ZZTest, Student. Two weeks shown.
GOOD TO KNOW
🔒Look-only. Nothing here changes attendance. Today's codes are fixed in Take Attendance; an older date — email Nicole.
📆The student's whole day — every period, not just your class. Dropped classes show too, for the days the student was in them.
🕐Today fills in as the day goes. A blank box today just means that period hasn't been marked yet.
⭐WISE points are in Behavior Support, so they're not on this page.
