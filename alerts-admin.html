The little icons by a student's name are alerts

Each one is a heads-up about that student. In PowerSchool, tap the icon and a pop-up tells you more. No icon = it doesn't apply to that student.

These are the ones Summit uses: (plus PowerSchool's own ).

GOOD TO KNOW
👁️Same icons on both sides. Teachers see them in PowerTeacher, the admin side sees them on the student pages — one source, no separate lists.
🤝Subs see the header (GOES BY, PRONOUNS) and the IEP and coverage icons, but not the pink Name & Pronouns alert.
THE ICONS · 1 OF 6

School vs. Home: Name & Pronouns

  • Means: the name and pronouns the student uses at school vs. the ones to use with family. On every student.
  • Tap it before anything goes home — progress report, report card, conference, phone call. “Same as at school” = no difference.
  • Everyday use: the header's GOES BY and PRONOUNS are enough.
  • Who enters it: the social workers. Subs never see this one.
  • Where: student → More → Student Custom Screens → Name & Pronouns (School vs. Home). Preferred First Name, Preferred Last Name and Pronouns feed the header; the two With Family fields feed the pop-up — fill both whenever anything family-facing differs, then Submit.
THE ICONS · 2 OF 6

IEP Accommodations & Modifications

  • Means: the student's IEP has been entered.
  • Tap it for: the testing accommodations (one per row — double time, breaks, tests read, calculator…) and the program modifications & accommodations, with the date of the IEP they come from.
  • Where it lives: student → More → Student Custom Screens → IEP Accommodations & Modifications (exactly what the pop-up shows). The full IEP file: Student Profile → Attachments.
  • Out of date after a new IEP? Reach out.

THE ICONS · 4 OF 6

Absences + Cuts (yellow person with a slash)

This icon appears once a student has 5 or more unexcused absences or cuts in any one class this year. Tap it for a breakdown by class:

  • Per / Course / Teacher: which class. HR1–HR4 is homeroom. WB1–WB4 is the work-based learning slot that runs during periods 1–4.
  • Unexcused column (A, CC, CG): absences and cuts only. Excused absences don't count here.
  • All absences column (incl. excused): every absence in that class, excused or not (A, CC, CG, EA, OSS, BD, ISR).
  • No-credit status column (30 absences): a student who reaches 30 total absences in a class for the term gets no credit for that course, excused or not.
    • Warning (orange) = 25–29 absences. Let the student's team know now (homeroom teacher, guidance counselor, social worker), while there's still time to step in.
    • NO CREDIT (red) = 30 or more.
  • Comes from: attendance, automatically — nothing to enter. It clears only if the attendance is corrected.
  • Leaving class with permission (WS, IH, TR) doesn't count as an absence, and neither do AP, WORK, FT, TEST or L.
THE ICONS · 5 OF 6

WBL — Work Schedule

  • Means: the student has work-based learning — a job site or Animal Care — during a WB block. In that block they are at work, not in class.
  • Tap it for: where they go, which day and periods, and the staff member — e.g. Walgreens — Tue, periods 2-4 | Shaina Rochester.
  • Comes from: the schedule, automatically — it appears with a WB-block section and goes away when that section is dropped.
  • Wrong site or day? That is a schedule fix — check with Shaina Rochester (WBL staff).
THE ICONS · 6 OF 6

Counselors (the three people)

  • Not an alert — PowerSchool's own icon, up by the student's name on every student.
  • Tap it for: the student's counselor.
  • The pin next to it (Locate) shows where the student is right now, according to their schedule.

ADD OR END A COVERAGE ALERT · 1 OF 3

Open the student's MBA Alert Creator page

C&C, Escort and Admin Support are manual alerts — someone puts the alert on the student when the coverage starts and ends it when the coverage stops. Nothing fills them in by itself.

  1. 1
    Search the student, then Student Profile → MBA Alert Creator.
  2. 2
    The top box, MBA Manual Alerts, lists the coverages the student has right now (none = empty list).
ADD OR END A COVERAGE ALERT · 2 OF 3

Add one — New Alert, fill the row, Save

  1. 1
    Click New Alert.
  2. 2
    Alert Type → AS, C&C or ESC.
  3. 3
    Alert → one line staff should read when they tap the icon (why, and anything they must do).
  4. 4
    Activation → leave blank to start today.
  5. 5
    Expires → the last day of the coverage, or blank if there is no end date yet.
  6. 6
    Click Save. Teachers and subs see the icon on the student right away.
ADD OR END A COVERAGE ALERT · 3 OF 3

End one — give it an Expires date

  1. 1
    Same page: click the pencil on the alert's row, put today's date in Expires, click Save.
  2. 2
    The icon disappears and the alert moves under Show Expired Alerts — so there is still a record of it.
  3. 3
    Delete removes it with no record — only for one added by mistake.
  4. 4
    The Daily Notes list is separate — update that too.
THE STUDENT HEADER

What each label in the header means

The header is the band of facts about the student at the top of every student page. Blank fields are hidden, so some students show fewer labels.

NAME · GOES BY · GOES BY (LAST) · PRONOUNS
NAME — the student's name in PowerSchool (Last, First). GOES BY — the first name the student uses at school. Use this one. GOES BY (LAST) — the last name they use. PRONOUNS — the student's pronouns at school (with family: see the pink alert).
YEAR LEVEL · STUDENT # · SCHOOL
YEAR LEVEL — PowerSchool's grade-level slot. At Summit it counts the student's year at Summit (1 = first year), not a school grade. STUDENT # — the student's number (the same as the state number now). SCHOOL — SSNYK for every active student. Admin side only — teachers do not see these three.
DOB · AGE
DOB — date of birth. AGE — years and months, worked out from the DOB.
HR · COUNSELOR · SW · SPEECH · OT · PSYCHIATRIST
HR homeroom teacher · COUNSELOR guidance counselor · SW social worker · SPEECH speech therapist · OT occupational therapist · PSYCHIATRIST psychiatrist. These are the people to loop in when something comes up with the student. Set where: SW, SPEECH, OT and PSYCHIATRIST on the student's Related Service Providers custom screen; COUNSELOR is PowerSchool's own counselor assignment (Student Profile → Modify Info → Counselors Assigned).
PLACEMENT · COTTAGE · DISTRICT
PLACEMENT — Day or Resident. COTTAGE — the cottage a residential student lives in (blank for day students). DISTRICT — the student's home school district, the one that placed them at Summit. Set where: Placement and cottage on the Placement Status custom screen.
