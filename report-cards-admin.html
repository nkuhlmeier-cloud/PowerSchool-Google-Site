GUIDANCE COUNSELORSMBA PLUGINS MENULOOK & PRINT ONLY
RUN REPORT CARDS  —  TAP A STEP
Report cards come from MBA Plugins → MBA Report Creator → Run Reports. Running and printing them never changes a grade.
1

Pick your students first (or skip it)

▾
On the Start Page, pick the students you want cards for — one student, a homeroom, or your caseload under Stored Searches. They show up under Current Student Selection. Printing for all of Summit? Skip this step.
Not sure how? See the Find a Student & Caseloads page of this site.
2

MBA Plugins → MBA Report Creator → Run Reports

▾
In the left menu, click MBA Plugins, then MBA Report Creator. Under Functions, click Run Reports. The page is called Report Creator Reports.
📷 picture 1 below
3

Fill in the one row

▾
Report: the report card for the quarter — for example SSNYK Report Card - Q1 2026-27.
Student Selection: Current Selection = the students from step 1 (the number shows how many). All Students in Summit School at Nyack = everyone.
Grade Level: All Grades. Sort Order: Student Name, or Home Room to print by homeroom.
Leave Include Dropped Courses and Include Transferred Out Students unchecked.
📷 picture 2 below
4

Click Generate Reports

▾
The blue Generate Reports button is at the end of the row. The cards open in a new tab. A big group takes a little while to load — wait for the buttons to show up in the box at the top left.
📷 picture 2 below
5

Click Print, then pick a printer or Save as PDF

▾
In the box at the top left, click Print. In the print window, Layout should already say Landscape and Margins Default. Pick your printer — or change Destination to Save as PDF.
📷 picture 3 below
GOOD TO KNOW
🔒Generate and Print never change anything. Run them as often as you like.
✋Skip Email and Archive Reports. Nicole saves each quarter's cards once — Email isn't used at Summit.
📅One report card per quarter. Pick the one that matches the quarter you're printing. New quarters show up in the Report list when they're ready.
👆Double-click a card to jump to that student's pages in PowerSchool.
✎️Wrong grade or comment on a card? Ask the teacher to fix it in PowerTeacher Pro, then generate the card again. Still wrong? Email Nicole.
